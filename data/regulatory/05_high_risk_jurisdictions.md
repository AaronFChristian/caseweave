# Typology: high-risk jurisdiction corridors

## Definition
Transfers to or from jurisdictions subject to international countermeasures,
enhanced due diligence requirements, or comprehensive sanctions warrant
elevated scrutiny. Geography alone is never sufficient grounds for suspicion,
but it raises the evidentiary bar the customer's explanation must meet.

## Observable indicators
- Repeated transfers to a listed jurisdiction with no corresponding trade
  documentation or evident commercial relationship.
- Escalating transfer values over a short period.
- Payment descriptions that are generic where the amounts are large, for
  example "consulting fee" or "services" without an invoice reference.
- Routing through an intermediary jurisdiction that has no connection to either
  the payer or the ultimate beneficiary.
- Payments to individuals where the stated purpose implies a corporate payee.

## Sanctions distinction
Screening against sanctions lists is a separate obligation from suspicious
activity monitoring and operates on a different legal basis. A sanctions match
requires immediate escalation and potential blocking; it is not resolved
through the suspicious activity reporting process.
