# Typology: dormant account reactivation

## Definition
An account with little or no activity for an extended period suddenly begins
transacting at a volume or value far above its historical baseline. Dormant
accounts are attractive to launderers because they carry an established
opening date and an apparently ordinary history.

## Observable indicators
- No activity for thirty days or more, followed by transactions many multiples
  of the prior average.
- Reactivation shortly after a change to contact details, address, or
  authorised signatories.
- Immediate outbound movement of newly credited funds.
- Reactivation across several dormant accounts sharing an address, device, or
  beneficial owner within the same period.

## Common benign explanations
Seasonal businesses, accounts held for a specific future purpose such as a
property purchase, inheritance receipts, insurance settlements, tax refunds and
accounts belonging to customers returning from extended travel or medical
absence all produce this pattern legitimately.
