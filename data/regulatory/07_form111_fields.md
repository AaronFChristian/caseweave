# Suspicious activity report: principal data elements

The filing form captures structured data alongside the narrative. An automated
drafting system must populate the structured fields as well as the prose, and
schema completeness should be validated before a filing is presented for
approval.

## Filing institution
Institution name, identifier, type, primary regulator, address, and the contact
office and telephone number for follow-up.

## Filing metadata
Report type, whether the filing is initial, corrected, continuing or a joint
filing; the prior report identifier where the filing continues an earlier one;
and the date the suspicious activity was detected, which starts the filing
deadline.

## Subject information
For each subject: full legal name and any alternate names, address, date of
birth, identifying document type and number, taxpayer identification number,
occupation or business type, contact details, relationship to the filing
institution, and whether the subject's identity was verified. Where a subject
is an entity, record the legal form and beneficial ownership if known.

## Suspicious activity information
Amount involved, the date range of the activity, the category or categories of
suspicious activity, the products and instruments involved, the account numbers
affected, and whether cumulative amounts across related filings are reported.

## Narrative
Free text. See the narrative guidance document.

## Timing
The report is generally due within thirty calendar days of initial detection of
facts that may constitute a basis for filing. Where no subject can be
identified, the period may be extended, but not beyond sixty days.
